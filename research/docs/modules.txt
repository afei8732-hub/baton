Modules overview
A module in Atlassian Forge is a component defined in your app's
manifest.yml
file. It specifies how your app integrates with Atlassian apps by defining properties and behaviors that correspond to specific extension points within these apps. Modules enable you to:
Extend functionality: Add custom features and integrations to Atlassian Cloud apps.
Interact with APIs: Utilize Atlassian's APIs to enhance app capabilities.
Customize user interfaces: Modify and extend the UI to fit your app's needs.
Depending on which Atlassian app you're building for, a number of different modules are available.
Forge
Explore modules that you can use with Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, and Compass to define functions, respond to app events, handle incoming third party webhook requests, and schedule periodic function execution.
Automation
Enable your app to integrate with Atlassian Automation to extend workflows and streamline processes.
Bitbucket
Extend Bitbucket with custom functionalities like enhanced repository management, automation of pull request workflows, and integration with external tools.
Compass
Enhance Compass by providing additional insights into component health, managing dependencies, and integrating with other developer tools.
Confluence
Add custom content, macros, and integrations to Confluence to improve documentation, collaboration, and information sharing capabilities.
Jira
Customize and extend Jira with additional fields, workflows, and interface elements to better support project management and issue tracking.
Jira Service Management
Add new features to Jira Service Management to improve how support teams handle customer requests, automate tasks, and connect with other tools for better service delivery.
Customer Service Management
Add new features to Customer Service Management to extend user and customer experiences across queues, request details, and the support site.
Rovo
Explore Forge Rovo modules to enhance your apps with advanced AI functionality.
Teamwork Graph
Connect your app to Atlassian’s Teamwork Graph to ingest, sync, and surface data from external sources.
Global module (EAP)
Give your app its own end-to-end, full-page experience across the Atlassian platform, accessed directly from the Atlassian app switcher.
